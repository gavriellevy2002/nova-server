# ✦ Nova — Command Center (blue core, rainbow tendrils)

Same two files. Drag **server.js** + **package.json** into
github.com/gavriellevy2002/nova-server → **Commit changes** → wait for **Live**.
The app self-updates on next open.

## The look
Tap **✦** for Command Center. Nova is a deep, glowing blue core — and out of it
erupt neural tendrils in every glowing neon colour: red, purple, gold, green,
cyan, magenta. The whole rainbow ring slowly turns and shimmers, glowing sparks
drift around her, and the departments on the left light up live as her agents
work a mission. The core stays a rich blue in every state (a touch brighter when
she listens); only the energy and the tendril storm change.

Everything else unchanged — voice, "Hey Nova," music, phone, email, missions.
Tap **✦** again for the normal chat.
